# Fluxquint™ Game Design Document

## Product identity

Fluxquint™ is a turn-based kinetic logic strategy game built around one causal system:

> Deterministic launch → magnetic capture → selective fusion → five-rank Quint → forecast gravity shift.

## Core rules

1. An incoming Core has rank 1–5. Standard seeded bags supply ranks 1–4; rank 5 is principally manufactured.
2. A captured Core may fuse with one orthogonally adjacent Core of the same rank.
3. Fusion advances the rank: 1+1→2, 2+2→3, 3+3→4 and 4+4→5.
4. Two rank-5 Cores create a Flux Burst and one Flux Choice.
5. A straight contiguous line containing ranks 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 exactly once is a Quint and clears.
6. An ordered 1–2–3–4–5 or 5–4–3–2–1 line is a Harmonic Quint.
7. Every five launches, forecast gravity compacts the board and can create Shift Quints.

## Modes

- **Endless Flux:** survive and maximise score.
- **Daily Quint:** one shared date seed and 25 launches.
- **Campaign:** seven guided chapters covering capture, fusion, Quints, gravity, harmony, Flux Bursts and mastery.
- **Creative Laboratory:** click cells to cycle ranks and test configurations.

## Scoring

Fusion rewards increase with rank. Basic Quints score 500, Harmonic Quints 1,000, Shift Quints receive a 50% bonus, Cross Quints receive a 75% bonus, and cascades increase by 50% per resolution stage.

## Design constraints

- No authoritative free-body physics.
- No hidden random outcomes after a launch is committed.
- No automatic fusion of untouched adjacent reserves.
- No dependency on colour or sound.
- No recognisable trade dress from 2048, Tetris, Angry Birds, Sudoku, Gomoku, Suika Game or Drop7.
