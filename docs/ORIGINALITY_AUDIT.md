# Fluxquint™ Originality Audit

## Distinctive combination

Fluxquint™ combines five linked operations on one persistent board:

1. aimed edge launch using quantised deterministic trajectories;
2. magnetic capture on an 8×8 lattice;
3. selective equal-rank fusion;
4. clearing a five-cell all-different line containing ranks 1–5;
5. predictable multidirectional gravity shifts.

## Deliberate separation from neighbouring games

- No global swipe-and-merge control or 4×4 presentation.
- No tetrominoes, conventional well or completed-row rule.
- No birds, slingshots, pigs or destructible tower imagery.
- No 9×9 regions or conventional Sudoku layout.
- No black-and-white stones or simple same-colour five-in-a-row victory.
- No fruit progression or free-physics container.
- No number-equals-contiguous-line-length clear rule.
